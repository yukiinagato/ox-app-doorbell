# iPad 1 / iOS 5 maintenance

The active, reviewed guidance is
[`docs/en/ios-compat-maintainer.md`](../docs/en/ios-compat-maintainer.md), with translations at
[`docs/ja/ios-compat-maintainer.md`](../docs/ja/ios-compat-maintainer.md) and
[`docs/zh/ios-compat-maintainer.md`](../docs/zh/ios-compat-maintainer.md).

Use `ios-compat` build and helper tooling. `ios-legacy` is archival. Never embed SSH passwords,
pairing keys, admin credentials, signing material, or other secrets in instructions or scripts.
The iPad 1 has a built-in microphone and no camera; outdoor installation limits are recorded in
the maintainer runbook. Historical debugging notes formerly kept here were device-session notes,
not a supported maintenance contract, and have been removed.
